# Project governance

lq-surface is community-contributed and maintained by lowquo. Contributions enter through pull requests, tests and maintainer review. Anyone can fork the MIT-licensed project; no assignment of copyright is required.

The official repository is protected by review and CI once its initial branch and workflows are established. The current lowquo maintainer commit identity is `dholdaway`; community commits retain their authorship in their forks and pull requests. No bot or assistant should make official commits while its author/committer attribution cannot be confirmed.

Issues and Discussions are the preferred forum for roadmap proposals. Maintainers reserve release signing, npm publishing and organisation settings. The project's current status is experimental 0.1.0, without a guaranteed release cadence.
