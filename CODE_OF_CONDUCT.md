# Code of Conduct

We want lq-surface to be a welcoming space for audio developers, designers and users.

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, threats, hate speech, doxxing and personal attacks are not acceptable. Respect privacy, different levels of experience and accessibility requirements.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove abusive content and restrict participation in proportion to the issue. To report a conduct concern, contact the maintainers privately using GitHub's repository contact or security reporting facility; do not publish personal allegations in an issue.

We will assess reports fairly, preserve privacy where practical and aim for constructive resolutions. This is a project-specific policy, not a claim to be the full Contributor Covenant text.
